## Detailed description (dashboard "Description" field, up to 16,000 chars)

Meet your AI writing assistant — a fast AI chatbot that lives in your browser's
side panel and helps you write better on any website, without switching tabs.

Answering emails, posting on social media, writing docs, or filling out forms?
Get instant AI help right where you're working:

✍️ Improve writing — rewrite, rephrase, and polish any text
✅ Fix grammar — correct spelling, grammar & punctuation in one click
📝 Summarize — turn long articles or threads into a quick summary
💡 Explain — get clear, simple explanations of anything
💬 AI chat — ask questions and get answers in plain text

Key features
• Side panel that stays open while you browse (no disappearing popup)
• Conversation modes: Chat, Improve Writing, Fix Grammar, Summarize, Explain
• Copy a reply or insert it straight into any text field on the page
• Markdown formatting, dark mode, and saved chat history
• Export conversations as Text, Markdown, or PDF
• Built-in daily usage / quota bar so you always know your limit
• Privacy-first: the API key stays server-side, never in the extension

Great as an AI writing helper, grammar checker, text summarizer, paraphrasing
tool, and an all-purpose AI assistant for Chrome.
